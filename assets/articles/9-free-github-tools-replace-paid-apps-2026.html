9 Free GitHub Tools That Replace Paid Apps (2026)

Every month, another subscription. An AI video tool here, a voice tool there, a scheduling app, a support inbox, a screen recorder. Each one is cheap on its own, and together they cost more than most creators earn from their content. This guide lists nine open-source tools from GitHub that replace nine paid apps, and every one of them is free to use.

These are the same nine tools from the Instagram reel Stop paying monthly. These 9 GitHub tools are FREE. The reel shows the list; this article gives you every link, what each tool does, and the quickest way to start. Star counts below are from 4 November 2026, and the links, licences and steps come from each project's own README on GitHub.

The 9 Tools at a Glance

  • 1. Open Higgsfield replaces Higgsfield (AI creation). github.com/wide-trace/open-higgsfield — 5.3k stars, no licence file. Start in the browser.
  • 2. VoiceStudio replaces ElevenLabs (voice AI). github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio — 99.9k stars, AGPL-3.0. Download the app.
  • 3. OpenSEO replaces Semrush (SEO). github.com/every-app/open-seo — 26.2k stars, MIT. Start in the browser.
  • 4. AppFlowy replaces Notion (workspace). github.com/AppFlowy-IO/AppFlowy — 78.1k stars, AGPL-3.0. Download the app.
  • 5. OpenWhispr replaces Wispr Flow (dictation). github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr — 10.6k stars, MIT. Download the app.
  • 6. OpenProject replaces Asana (project management). github.com/opf/openproject — 17.0k stars, GPL-3.0. Start in the browser.
  • 7. Cal.diy replaces Calendly (scheduling). github.com/calcom/cal.diy — 49.7k stars, MIT. Self-host only.
  • 8. Chatwoot replaces Intercom (support). github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot — 38.8k stars, MIT. Self-host.
  • 9. Cap replaces Loom (screen recording). github.com/CapSoftware/Cap — 24.3k stars, AGPLv3 (mostly). Download the app.

Which Way Should You Start?

Pick the path that feels easiest today. You can always switch later. Four tools have ready installers you download and run: VoiceStudio, AppFlowy, OpenWhispr and Cap, for Mac, Windows and Linux. Three tools run in the browser with nothing to install: Open Higgsfield and OpenSEO on their own websites, and OpenProject through a free cloud trial. Two tools are self-hosted: Cal.diy is self-hosted only, and Chatwoot deploys in one click on Heroku or DigitalOcean.

1. Open Higgsfield: AI Creation, Replaces Higgsfield

Open Higgsfield is an image and video generation studio. One prompt bar, 38 models (8 image, 30 video), each model with its own settings, and every finished run saved in one gallery. The studio itself is free: you generate with your own platform API key. The easiest start is the browser: open openhiggsfield.ai, press Add key, and paste your platform key in the form id:secret. Generation is billed by that platform, not by the studio, so this one is free to open and pay-per-use to generate.

2. VoiceStudio: Voice AI, Replaces ElevenLabs

VoiceStudio is a fully local ElevenLabs alternative that runs on your own computer: voice cloning, voice design (describe a voice in words), video dubbing, dictation, transcription and audiobooks in 646 languages. You download only the models you choose, and your voice work stays on your machine. On Mac, install with Homebrew or the one-command installer (curl -fsSL https://voicestudio.sh/install | sh), or grab the DMG from the releases page. On Windows, run the installer in PowerShell (irm https://voicestudio.sh/install.ps1 | iex) or download the .exe from the releases page.

3. OpenSEO: SEO, Replaces Semrush

OpenSEO is a pay-as-you-go alternative to Semrush and Ahrefs: keyword research, rank tracking, competitor insights, backlinks, site audits and AI visibility. There is no subscription. You bring your own DataForSEO API key and pay only for what you use, and it also has an MCP server so Claude Code, ChatGPT or Codex can use your SEO data. Make an account at dataforseo.com for the key, then try it free at openseo.so, or self-host with Docker or Cloudflare. The guides are in the repo's docs folder.

4. AppFlowy: Workspace, Replaces Notion

AppFlowy is the leading open-source Notion alternative: an AI workspace for docs, wikis, databases, kanban boards and team projects, where you keep control of your data. Download the app for macOS, Windows or Linux from the GitHub releases page (also on Flathub and Snapcraft), or get it on the App Store and Play Store for mobile. Open AppFlowy, create your workspace, and add pages: notes and docs, a database for tasks, or a kanban board for to-dos. You can self-host it for your team when you outgrow the desktop app.

5. OpenWhispr: Dictation, Replaces Wispr Flow

OpenWhispr is voice-to-text dictation for any app. Press a hotkey, speak, and your words appear at your cursor. Choose fully private offline models or cloud models with your own key. It also does dictation translation, meeting transcription and notes, on macOS, Windows and Linux. Download the .dmg (Mac), .exe (Windows) or .AppImage, .deb or .rpm (Linux) from the latest release. On Mac you can also use Homebrew: brew install --cask openwhispr. Then pick local or cloud, and start dictating anywhere you can type.

6. OpenProject: Project Management, Replaces Asana

OpenProject is open-source project management for teams: projects and portfolios, agile boards (Kanban, Scrum), Gantt-chart planning, task and bug tracking, time tracking and budgets, wikis and meeting minutes. The repo calls itself a Jira alternative, and it covers what teams use Asana for too. Try it without installing: start a 14-day free cloud trial at start.openproject.com. For free forever, download the free Community edition and follow the install guide (Docker or packages).

7. Cal.diy: Scheduling, Replaces Calendly

Cal.diy is the community, 100% MIT-licensed version of Cal.com: scheduling where people book a free slot from your calendar through your booking link. It is a fork of Cal.com with the enterprise code removed, so it needs no licence key. Note this one clearly: Cal.diy is self-hosted only. There is no hosted version. It needs Docker with docker compose. Clone the repo (git clone --recursive https://github.com/calcom/cal.diy.git), copy .env.example to .env, and add two secret keys, NEXTAUTH_SECRET and CALENDSO_ENCRYPTION_KEY, generated with openssl rand -base64 32 and openssl rand -base64 24.

8. Chatwoot: Support, Replaces Intercom

Chatwoot is an open-source customer support platform, an alternative to Intercom and Zendesk. One inbox for website live chat, email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and more, plus the Captain AI agent for common questions, a help-center portal, canned responses, labels and auto-assignment. Deploy it in one click on Heroku or DigitalOcean using the buttons in the README, or pick another option on the deploy page at chatwoot.com/deploy. Then connect your channels: add an inbox for each place customers reach you.

9. Cap: Screen Recording, Replaces Loom

Cap is the open-source Loom alternative: record your screen, camera and mic, then share a link or export a polished video. It has comments, transcripts, viewer analytics and AI titles, summaries and chapters, and it can import your old Loom videos. Download the desktop app from cap.so/download, then sign in. Instant Mode uploads while you record and gives you a share link the moment you stop. Studio Mode records locally and opens the editor for backgrounds, zooms, trimming and captions.

Two Honest Warnings Before You Start

First, two of these tools are free to open but pay-per-use to run. Open Higgsfield needs your own generation-platform key, and OpenSEO needs a DataForSEO key. Both are billed by the data provider per use, not by the tool, so they replace the subscription but not the usage cost. Second, install commands can change. If a step in this guide fails, check the repo's README first: it is the source of truth, and it is updated by the people who maintain the tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all nine tools really free?

Yes, all nine are open source and free to use or self-host, with no subscription for the tool itself. Two of them, Open Higgsfield and OpenSEO, need your own API key, and the key provider bills per use. The rest cost nothing to download and run.

Do I need to self-host any of these?

Only Cal.diy, which is self-hosted only and has no hosted version. Chatwoot can be self-hosted but also deploys in one click. The other seven either download as apps or run in the browser.

Which tool is the easiest to start with?

Cap and AppFlowy: download the app and you are using it in minutes. Open Higgsfield and OpenSEO are just as fast in the browser, but you need an API key first. Start with one tool, the one that replaces the subscription you pay the most for, and add the rest one at a time.

Are open-source tools safe to use?

These are public projects with thousands of stars and public code that anyone can read. Download them from their official GitHub releases pages or official websites, keep them updated, and read each repo's README before you self-host anything that faces the internet.

Nine paid apps, nine free replacements, zero subscriptions. Start with the one that saves you the most money this month: for most creators that is the voice tool, the workspace, or the screen recorder. Download it today, cancel one subscription, and the saving is real from the first month.
