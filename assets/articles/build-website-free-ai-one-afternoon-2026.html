Build a Whole Website With Free AI in One Afternoon

You describe a website in plain English. An AI builder writes the code, lays out the pages and publishes it. One afternoon is enough for a real, working site: a portfolio, a small business page, or a landing page for your service. You do not need to learn to code. You do need to pick the right builder, because “free” means something different on every one of them. This guide compares the free plans honestly, then gives you an afternoon plan that actually finishes.

The builders below all turn a prompt into a working site. They differ in what the free plan lets you publish, how much AI you get, and whether your site carries their branding. Read the comparison first, pick one, and stay with it for the afternoon. Switching builders halfway is how the afternoon disappears.

Pick Your Builder First

  • Lovable — describe a full web app in plain English and get the database, auth and publishing handled for you. The free plan is $0 with no credit card needed, with a small daily credit allowance (listed as 5 credits a day in November 2026 comparisons) and up to 5 lovable.app domains. Best if you want a finished product with as little code as possible. lovable.dev
  • Bolt.new — a full-stack app built and run entirely in the browser, with hosting and databases included. The free plan is $0 with a 300K token daily limit and 1M tokens a month, and free sites carry Bolt branding. Best if you want everything in one browser tab. bolt.new
  • v0 — describe an interface and get working code you can deploy to Vercel. The free plan is $0 with a limit of 7 messages a day, plus visual editing and GitHub sync. Best if you want clean code you can keep and extend. v0.app
  • Framer — a design canvas with built-in hosting, CMS, SEO and analytics. The free plan publishes on a free framer.app domain with 1 GB of bandwidth and 500 AI credits to try. A custom domain needs a paid plan, and Framer describes the free plan as ideal for non-commercial use. framer.com
  • Durable — the fastest route to a live local-service site. The $0 plan publishes on a durable.site subdomain, keeps Durable branding, and includes 5 AI images and 10 AI chat messages a month. durable.co
  • Canva — build a simple visual site and publish it free at an address like xyz.my.canva.site, with a maximum of 5 live free-domain websites. Best for a simple event, profile or information page. canva.com
  • Wix — AI site creation with a free start. A Premium plan is required to connect a custom domain or collect online payments. wix.com

Plan details change often. The figures above come from pricing pages and comparisons checked in October and November 2026. Before you build, open the builder’s own pricing page and confirm the free limits. A detailed comparison of Lovable, Bolt and v0 is at getstackdrop.com/compare/lovable-vs-bolt-vs-v0.

The Afternoon Plan

  • Hour 1 — prompt and generate. Write one clear prompt (see below), paste it into the builder, and let it generate the first version. Do not judge the first draft. It is a starting point, not the site.
  • Hour 2 — fix the words and pictures. Replace the placeholder text with your real words, swap in your own photos, and check the site on your phone. Most AI sites look good on a laptop and break on a phone. Fix the phone view before anything else.
  • Hour 3 — publish and test. Publish on the free subdomain, open the live link on your phone, click every button, and send the link to one friend. If a button goes nowhere, fix it now. A site with one dead button looks worse than a site with one less section.

Write a Prompt That Works

A good builder prompt has four parts: who the site is for, what it should do, what sections it needs, and how it should look. Example:

“Build a portfolio website for a video editor in Pakistan. It needs a home section with a short intro, a services section with video editing, thumbnails and UGC ads, a work gallery with six video cards, and a contact section with Instagram and WhatsApp buttons. Style: black and gold, premium, clean, easy to read on a phone.”

Keep it to one paragraph. If the first result misses a section, ask for that section in one follow-up message instead of rewriting the whole prompt. Follow-up messages are how the free credit allowance disappears fastest, so make each one count.

What Free Really Means

Free has four usual limits. Your address is the builder’s subdomain, not your own domain. The builder’s branding stays on the site. Your AI usage is capped, daily or monthly. And selling or taking payments usually needs a paid plan. None of that makes a free site useless: a live site on a free subdomain still shows your work, still takes enquiries, and still beats having no site at all. It only means you should know the limits before you promise a client a finished website.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for a live site on the builder’s subdomain. Lovable, Bolt, v0, Framer, Durable, Canva and Wix all have a $0 route to a published site. Hostinger is different: its AI builder is a 14-day free trial, not a free plan.

Can I connect my own domain later?

Yes, on a paid plan. Every builder above keeps custom domains behind a paid tier. Start free, and upgrade only when the site starts earning.

Who owns my website?

You own your content. The builder hosts it. If you ever want to leave, export what you can: your text, your images and, on tools like v0, your code. Check the export options before you build a site you plan to keep for years.

Can I take payments on a free site?

No, not on the free plans listed here. Wix requires Premium for online payments, and the other builders keep checkout behind paid tiers too. For a free site, take enquiries through WhatsApp or a form instead.

The Bottom Line

Pick one builder, write one clear prompt, and publish the same afternoon. Framer or Durable if you want the fastest live site. Lovable or Bolt if you want a fuller app. v0 if you want the code. Start free, share the link, and upgrade only when the site starts earning.
