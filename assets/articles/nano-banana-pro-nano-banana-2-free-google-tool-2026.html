Nano Banana Pro and Nano Banana 2 Free Through a Google Tool

Most people think Nano Banana Pro and Nano Banana 2 are paid-only. They are not. A Google tool gives you access to these powerful AI image models for free, and you can create professional AI images, thumbnails, product photos, and social media creatives without paying.

This guide is based on the Instagram reel Google Ke Premium AI Tools FREE Mein?. The Google tool is Google Flow, and you can see it right on the reel's own cover.

Which Model Is Which

Two models, two jobs. Nano Banana Pro is Gemini 3 Pro Image, Google's most powerful image model: the best quality, the most detail, the one for final work. Nano Banana 2 is Gemini 3.1 Flash Image: faster, lighter, and the one for quick drafts and variations. Pro is the flagship; 2 is the fast one.

The Google Tool: Google Flow

Google Flow is Google's AI filmmaking and image tool, and its image generation runs on Nano Banana Pro. Flow gives free users up to 20 Nano Banana Pro images a day. The Gemini app gives you Nano Banana 2 for free as well. That is the whole secret: the premium models are inside Google's own free tools.

How To Use It, Step by Step

Open Google Flow in your browser and sign in with your Google account. Start a new image, choose the text-to-image option, and write a detailed prompt: what the image shows, the style, the lighting, the mood. Press Generate. Flow creates the image with Nano Banana Pro, and you can download it straight away.

Write prompts like briefs, not wishes. "A product photo of a black watch on a dark stone surface, soft studio light, premium feel" will beat "nice watch photo" every time. If the first result is close but not right, change one thing in the prompt and generate again.

What To Make With It

  • Thumbnails: bold, high-detail YouTube and Instagram thumbnails that stand out in a feed.
  • Product photos: clean studio-style shots of a product, without a studio.
  • Social creatives: posters, banners, and post images in any style you describe.
  • Concept art: quick visual ideas for a video, a brand, or a project before you commit to production.

The Free Limits, Honestly

Flow's free allowance is up to 20 Nano Banana Pro images a day, and the Gemini app covers Nano Banana 2 for free. Limits can change, so check Flow's own counter before you plan a big batch. Twenty a day is plenty for a creator: that is a week of thumbnails in a single afternoon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nano Banana Pro really free in Flow?

Yes, up to 20 images a day on the free tier. Beyond that, Flow's paid plans apply.

What is the difference between Pro and 2?

Pro, Gemini 3 Pro Image, is the highest quality model. 2, Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, is faster and lighter. Use Pro for finals, 2 for drafts.

Can I use the images commercially?

Google's terms cover AI-generated images from its tools, but read the current terms on Flow's site before using images in paid ads or client work.

The Bottom Line

Open Google Flow, write a good prompt, and generate with Nano Banana Pro for free. The premium models were never locked away. They were sitting inside Google's own free tool, waiting to be used.
