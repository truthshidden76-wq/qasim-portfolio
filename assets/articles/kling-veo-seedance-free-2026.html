Kling, Google Veo and Seedance 2 are the premium AI video models everyone talks about, and all of them are paywalled on their official sites. Website 1: The aggregator with trial credits

The first site bundles multiple premium models, including Kling, behind one free trial balance. You get a starter credit pack on signup with no card required. Spend it on your most important generations first, because trial credits do not renew monthly on most aggregators.

Website 2: The promotional tier

The second platform runs a generous free tier as a growth strategy, including access to Veo-class models at reduced resolution. Free renders are capped daily and queued behind paying users, so generate overnight or during off-peak hours for faster turnaround.

Website 3: The community platform

The third option is a creator community site that subsidises generations, including Seedance 2 access, in exchange for public sharing. Your renders may be visible to other users, so use it for portfolio pieces and experiments, not client-confidential work.

Maximising free access

Create accounts on all three, track each one's credit balance in a spreadsheet, and route each generation to whichever platform fits best. Draft at low resolution, then spend premium credits only on final renders. This discipline stretches free access for months.

What to watch out for

Free access terms change without notice. Never build a client deadline around a free tier alone, and always export and back up your best generations the same day you make them.
